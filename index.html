The second, also IYIA KERR but with no IPI number, carries 59 works and was last updated in February 2024 — your earlier registrations, including the CO3, Mac Critter and Fox BD placements. An IPI number is how every society and administrator recognises you as one writer. Works sitting on a record without one are harder to tie back to you, and any future claim or dispute on them starts from a weaker identity. Asking The MLC to consolidate the two records under your IPI is the first clean-up item, and it costs nothing.
Your publisher entity at The MLC, IYIA KERR (HFA publisher number P359J7), carries IPI 01151327590 — the same number as your writer record. A writer and the publishing entity that represents them normally each carry their own IPI number, so one of the two records was most likely filed with the wrong number. Your society can tell you which number belongs to you as a writer and which to your publishing designee; that answer decides which record gets corrected.
